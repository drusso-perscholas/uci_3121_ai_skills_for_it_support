Spec: connect a contractor's tablet to CorpWiFi

A senior tech closed a similar CorpWiFi ticket last week, for a contractor's tablet instead of a laptop. Here is the spec they wrote before prompting.

Audience

Tier 1 tech

Why this matters Without an audience, the AI does not know how much to explain, so it either assumes expert knowledge or over-explains basics a tech already knows.

Task

Document how to connect a company-issued tablet to CorpWiFi.

Why this matters A vague task lets the AI guess what "done" means. Naming the exact action keeps the draft from wandering into related but unwanted territory.

Required steps

  1. Open Wi-Fi settings on the tablet.
  2. Select CorpWiFi from the list of available networks.
  3. Enter the network password when prompted.
  4. Confirm the tablet shows "Connected" and can load a webpage.
Why this matters Writing the steps you believe are involved forces you to think through the sequence before the AI invents its own order, so you have something concrete to check step by step against the real system later.

Success state

The tablet connects and the user can reach the internet.

Why this matters Without this, the AI does not know when the article is done, so it pads or stops early.
