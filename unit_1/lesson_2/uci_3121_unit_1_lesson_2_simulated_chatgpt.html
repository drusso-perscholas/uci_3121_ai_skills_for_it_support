You are a senior IT support technician writing a knowledge base
article for Tier 1 staff. Write the article from the
SPECIFICATION below.

SPECIFICATION:
Audience: Tier 1 support tech with no prior context on this ticket.
Task: Document how to connect a contractor's tablet to CorpWiFi.
Required steps:
1. Open Wi-Fi settings on the tablet.
2. Select CorpWiFi from the list of available networks.
3. Enter the network passphrase.
4. Confirm the tablet shows as connected and can reach the internet.
Success state: The tablet connects and the user can reach the internet.

OUTPUT FORMAT:
1. The article in clear numbered steps.
2. Then a section titled ASSUMPTIONS I MADE listing every menu
   label, path, version, or fact you assumed but did not verify.
3. Then a section titled WHAT TO CHECK listing anything a reader
   could get wrong.
